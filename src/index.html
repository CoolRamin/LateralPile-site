Our software

Two programs, one family.

Each has its own website with everything about it: what it computes, the standards it follows, how to get it and how to reach us about it.

FoundaTrix Studio

Analysis and design of rigid foundations

Any rigid foundation, modelled in 3D: spread, combined, strapped and mat foundations of any outline, carrying any number of pedestals, and pile caps on PileAxis piles.

  • Bearing pressure under every load combination, partial uplift included, with overturning and sliding.
  • Concrete and anchor rods designed to ACI 318-19; steel stands and racks to AISC 360-22.
  • Wizards for vessels, heat exchangers, substation equipment and pipe supports.
Visit foundatrix.com Get it from the Microsoft Store

PileAxis

Lateral and axial analysis of piles and drilled shafts

A single pile or drilled shaft in layered soil, analysed under combined lateral and axial load and checked from the soil to the section.

  • The p-y and t-z response solved together for every load combination.
  • The section checked to ACI 318-25 on a confined-concrete fibre model, with independent soil and equilibrium audits.
  • Drilled shafts, and as a Pro feature precast pretensioned piles and steel H-piles.
Visit pileaxis.com Coming soon to the Microsoft Store

They work together. One account signs in to both, and they share one cloud: a project can hold pile and foundation designs side by side, and a FoundaTrix Studio pile cap stands on pile models taken from PileAxis designs in the same project.

Our approach

Comprehensive, accurate and complete.

Specialized software earns its place by doing the whole job well. These are the commitments every RamisoftSolutions program is built to.

Comprehensive

The whole design problem in one program: the loads, the analysis, every strength and serviceability check, and the report, without hand-offs to spreadsheets in between.

Engineering accuracy

Methods implemented from the standards themselves and held, by automated tests, to closed-form solutions and to the worked examples published with them.

Completeness

Every check reported, passing or failing, with the governing case, the equation and the provision it comes from. Nothing is hidden behind a single verdict.

Checked from every side

Results are audited independently of the solution that produced them: equilibrium closed, the soil's mobilisation measured, the assumptions stated where they apply.

Free to start

The core analysis and design in each program is free, needs no account and works offline. Sign in only for the cloud and the Pro capabilities.

Your work stays yours

No advertising, no analytics, no telemetry. Projects on your computer never leave it unless you save them to the cloud.
